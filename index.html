Hermes Google App

A personal-use OAuth application.

This application connects a self-hosted Hermes agent to Google Drive, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, Contacts, and Gmail on behalf of a single user: the account owner.

What it does

It lets its owner ask an AI assistant to read and write files in their own Google Drive, create and edit Docs and Sheets, view and create Calendar events, and read or send Gmail, all on the owner's own account, at the owner's request.

Who uses it

One person. It is not distributed or offered as a service, and has no users other than the account owner.

Data handling

Data is read only from the owner's own Google account, on the owner's own machine. Nothing is shared with third parties, and no analytics or tracking is performed.
